# inv-011: Sell's receipt heading is covered by the docked drawer
## Question
Why does bug-002/N2 FAIL the e2e gate at attempt 2 (wb-sell's receipt heading "Receipt for rung 4: what was sent, what came back" is in the DOM but covered by the sticky requests drawer in the regression screenshots), and is the cause in the sell screen's region composition, in the kit drawer's sticky docking, or in tests that check the heading exists but not that it is visible?
## Hypothesis
The sell inspector section has no dock wrapper, so the kit drawer (`position: sticky; bottom: 0`) takes that section
as its sticky range and rides up over its sibling h2; the sell test checks only that the h2 exists and labels the
section. Disproved if the drawer's box never meets the h2's box at any scroll, the h2 is hidden by CSS, or only the
full-page capture shows it.
## Evidence
- `docs/trace/bug-002/N2/loop.jsonl`, gate.e2e at 02d7dee9: 805 passed, 96 failed, regression only (main's 65 plus
  wb-sell x15, wb-cancel x16).
- `src/Uinsure.Policies.Web/test-results/regression-wb-sell-*` and `regression-wb-cancel-*`: actual, expected and
  diff PNGs at 1440 and 390, light and dark.
- `docs/ui/screens/wb-sell/populated.html`, `submitting.html`, `api-unreachable.html`; `docs/ui/screens/wb-cancel/`.
- `docs/trace/bug-002/N2/findings/A1.md` (round A1) and `findings/A2.md` (this round).
## Findings
Yes: the cause is the sell screen's region composition, with the sell test letting it through. Not the kit drawer.
See [findings/A2.md](../../trace/bug-002/N2/findings/A2.md).
- I1 (WB-7): `SellPolicy.tsx:406-409` puts the drawer straight in the receipt section after its h2, so the drawer's
  sticky range (`InspectorDrawer.module.css:2-5`) is the whole section and it rides over the h2. Cancel
  (`CancelPolicy.tsx:318-321`) and claim (`RecordClaim.tsx:253-255`) have the dock; sell never got one, because A1 I7
  directed the dock for cancel only and the signed-off wb-sell has none. Recurring in mechanism (A1 I3, I7), new in
  this file.
- I2 (WB-7): `e2e/N2/wb-7.spec.ts:260-268` checks the sell heading's presence, not that it is visible, and not the
  page length. Cancel has both (:295-333). New.
- I3 (HONEST-1): `app/data/api.ts:428-447` leaves a logged exchange at status null when fetch rejects, and
  `RequestsDrawer.tsx:29` shows it as "Waiting" in both api-unreachable states. A status the API never gave.
  The behaviour comes from before N2; N2's open drawer makes it visible. New.
- I4 (not a defect): the wb-cancel diffs are exactly the signed-off inspector, with a session-log count above the
  signed-off one (the on-open read, a delegated A1 ruling). 12 cancel shots are baselineable now. The 4
  api-unreachable shots wait for I3. No wb-sell shot is baselineable until I1. Sell's submitting and api-unreachable
  regions draw a note, not the drawer, in the signed-off pages.
- I5 (not a defect): the dock's `overflow: clip` changes nothing visible on claim. wb-claim's regression passes at
  02d7dee9.
